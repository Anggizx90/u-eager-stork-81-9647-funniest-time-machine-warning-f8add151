# Candidates — Funniest Time Machine Warning Label (<=120 chars)

Judge brief, in order: (1) must be a time-machine warning label; (2) turn a real
hazard — paradox, duplicate self, stranded ancestor — into the joke, not name it
flatly; (3) surprise saved for the last few words; (4) straight face, like a
genuine safety notice; (5) no wasted words.

## Drafts (char counts verified in Python)

1. `WARNING: Do not introduce yourself to ancestors. One will marry your
   grandmother, and you will be your own grandfather.` — 119 chars.
   Paradox (own-grandfather), surprise at the very end, straight-face WARNING.
2. `CAUTION: If you meet yourself, do not lend yourself money. You have never
   once paid yourself back.` — 98 chars. Duplicate self + debt; the punch is
   the last clause; tight.
3. `WARNING: Do not kill your grandfather. He holds your birth certificate,
   and he knows it.` — 88 chars. Paradox hazard, dry threat, surprise at end.
4. `WARNING: Meeting yourself voids the warranty. Both of you will be charged,
   and neither of you will remember why.` — 112 chars. Straight-face
   bureaucracy; duplicate self as a billing problem.
5. `CAUTION: Your past self is not a tour guide. Do not ask it for directions.
   It is lost, and it is you.` — 106 chars. Duplicate self; last three words
   are the turn.
6. `NOTICE: Arriving before you left is normal. Introducing yourself to your
   grandfather is not. He will marry her first.` — 113 chars. Muddy: "her"
   is unclear, weakens the paradox.
7. `WARNING: Exiting before you board may strand you in 1348. Do not sneeze.
   The plague is listening.` — 100 chars. Stranded hazard, but the joke is
   atmosphere, not a paradox turn.
8. `WARNING: Do not wave at your ancestors. One waves back, marries your
   grandmother, and now you are your own uncle.` — 108 chars. The payoff
   does not follow: marrying grandmother makes him a grandfather, not an
   uncle. Logically broken — rejected.
9. `NOTICE: If you arrive before you depart, please do not applaud yourself.
   You have not done anything yet.` — 109 chars. Flat: states the paradox
   instead of turning it into a joke.
10. `WARNING: Do not shake hands with yourself. One of you is lying about the
    future, and it is the older one.` — 104 chars. Duplicate self; the sting
    is the last clause, but "lying about the future" is vague.
11. `CAUTION: Time travel may cause you to meet yourself. Please do not
    introduce yourselves. You already know how this ends.` — 113 chars.
    Decent, but "You already know how this ends" is a stock line.
12. `WARNING: Your grandfather is not a photo opportunity. Do not pose with
    him. He has not met your grandmother yet.` — 104 chars. Stranded
    ancestor; the last sentence is a genuine paradox turn, straight-faced.

## Head-to-head (through the real program, top 3)

- #1 (119): strongest paradox, cleanest logic, surprise is the final phrase.
  The whole label is a warning; the joke is the consequence.
- #2 (98): funniest single image (lending money to yourself), but the hazard
  is a debt, not a time-travel paradox — weaker on criterion 2.
- #12 (104): good turn, but "photo opportunity" is a weaker setup than #1's
  "introduce yourself", and the payoff is a fact rather than a consequence.

## Winner: #1

`WARNING: Do not introduce yourself to ancestors. One will marry your
grandmother, and you will be your own grandfather.`

Why it wins: it is a genuine warning label in form (WARNING + imperative);
the hazard is the classic grandfather paradox, but the joke is not the
paradox named — it is the consequence, delivered as a bureaucratic fact in
the last six words; nothing is wasted (119 of 120 chars, every clause does
work); and the surprise is saved for the very end, where the reader least
expects a family-tree verdict on a safety notice.
